Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how this Discord bot (“the Bot”) handles information when operating inside Discord servers.

Summary

  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We minimize data collection to what’s necessary to run commands and keep the Bot functional.
  • You can remove the Bot at any time to stop all processing within your server.

What data do we process?

The Bot processes only the data that Discord transmits to enable commands, such as user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and message/interaction content that triggers a command. For music features, the Bot may temporarily handle track URLs/titles to play audio.

Do we store data?

By default, the Bot does not persist personal data. If optional features require short-lived storage (e.g., queue state, premium guild settings), the data is kept only as long as necessary for the feature and is deleted when no longer needed. We do not create user profiles.

Third‑party services

The Bot runs on Discord’s platform and may interact with lawful, licensed audio sources. Your use of Discord is governed by the Discord Terms and Privacy Policy.

Children’s privacy

The Bot is intended for communities that meet Discord’s minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age allowed by Discord in your region.

Data requests

Server owners or authorized admins can contact us to request deletion of configuration data specific to their server. Because we avoid persistent storage of personal data, there is typically nothing to export.
